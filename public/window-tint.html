The wrong film can crack your glass. We make sure that never happens.

Most tint problems don't come from bad film — they come from the wrong film on the wrong glass. Dark film on the wrong pane absorbs heat unevenly, and the glass can crack from thermal stress. On dual-pane windows it can break the seal and fog the window permanently.

That's why ikonic never quotes blind. Before you get a number, we identify your exact glass — single or dual-pane, tempered or annealed, coated or clear — and confirm your film is approved on the manufacturer's own glass-to-film compatibility chart. If a combination isn't safe, we'll tell you, and spec one that is.

It takes a few extra minutes. It's the difference between a quote and a guess.

Why Colorado glass needs this

Colorado sits at altitude, with strong sun and wide swings between hot, sun-baked afternoons and cold nights. Glass here takes more abuse than glass in most of the country — and as Section 2 explains, that's exactly the kind of thermal stress that makes the wrong film-to-glass match a real risk, not a theoretical one. It's also why we check your glass before we quote instead of after.

Frequently asked questions

Will my rooms feel darker?

Modern solar films are engineered to cut heat and glare, not light. Many of our most-installed films are barely visible once applied. If you want privacy or a decorative look we can go darker or frosted — but that's a choice, not a side effect.

Can window film crack my glass?

The wrong film on the wrong glass can — that's exactly why we identify your glass and verify the film against the manufacturer's compatibility chart before we quote. If a combination isn't safe, we won't sell it to you.

Do you tint dual-pane windows?

Often, yes — with films specifically rated for insulated glass, confirmed against the manufacturer's chart. Some dual-pane situations aren't safe for any film, and we'll tell you that honestly at the assessment.

How long does installation take?

Most homes and storefronts are finished in a single visit. We work by appointment at your building.

How much does it cost?

Every quote is written and priced by the job, because glass area, film type, and access differ. Tell us about your project with the 2-minute form and you'll have an exact written number within one business day.

Does film help with energy bills?

Solar-control film reduces the heat load coming through your glass, which reduces what your cooling has to fight.
